# Provider marks

These locally bundled marks identify connection providers. They are trademarks of their respective owners and do not indicate affiliation with zQ. No logo requests leave the app at runtime: Vite's `?inline` imports embed every asset in the chat module bundle.

`ProviderLogo({ provider, size = 18, className })` is decorative (`aria-hidden`); its adjacent provider name or containing control must supply an accessible name. A CSS alpha mask renders the monochrome marks in the surrounding text color, making them legible on neutral light and dark surfaces. Gemini and vLLM retain their source colors. Bedrock uses the same theme-aware monochrome treatment as the other single-color marks. The Groq mark remains available for existing saved connections. Dimensions, aspect ratio, and theme behavior are self-contained; no extra stylesheet is required. No new controls or context menus are introduced by this component.
